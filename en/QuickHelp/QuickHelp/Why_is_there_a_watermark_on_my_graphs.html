6.3 FAQ-4 Why is there a watermark on my graphs?

Graph-Watermark

Last Update: 9/27/2024


The trial version of Origin displays watermarks on all graphs, including those that are exported and copied. Once a license is purchased, you can convert your trial version to the full product and the watermarks will no longer appear.

If you have a purchased license and you are still getting a watermark, please try the following:

  1. Close Origin if it is open
  2. Right-click the Origin icon and select Run as administrator
  3. In the software, go to Help: Activate License and click Activate once more

You may restart Origin normally afterwards.
