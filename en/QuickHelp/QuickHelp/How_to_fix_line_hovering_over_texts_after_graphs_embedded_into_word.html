1.302 FAQ-1203 Lines that appear hovering over text of graphs after the graphs are embedded into Microsoft Word. How can I fix it?

Line-Hovering-over-Texts

Last Update: 8/19/2024

We have received reports that in Origin 2024, lines appear to be hovering over the text of graphs after they are embedded into Microsoft Word. However, this issue has been resolved in Origin 2024b.

Please upgrade your Origin to version 2024b or a later version to fix the problem.
