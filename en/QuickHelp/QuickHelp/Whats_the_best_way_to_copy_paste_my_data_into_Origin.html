2.139 FAQ-1216 What’s the best way to copy paste my data into Origin?

Copy-Paste-Data-to-Origin

 

Last Update: 4/15/2025

Most of the experimental datasets begin with a text header and follow by numeric data. When you copy such datasets into a Origin worksheet, it is highly recommended to select the first cell of the target columns, right click and select Paste (or Ctrl+V). The text line will be auto detected as header and moved to Long Name.

Keywords:Copy, Ctrl+C, Paste, Ctrl+V, copy from Excel, clipboard, data with header line
