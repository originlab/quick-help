5.93 FAQ-1221 When I open Project Explorer, the folders are visible but the windows in the folders are no longer visible. Why?

Window-Icons-Disappear-in-PE

 

Last Update: 7/7/2025

In the older versions prior to Origin 2025b, sometimes the window list would not appear in the bottom panel of Project Explorer even though there were multiple windows under the current folder.

This is because the information columns have been hidden in the bottom panel, you can right-click on the header to check one information column or choose Show All to show them back.

Note: Since Origin 2025b, Origin will already retain one information column at least to avoid this problem.
